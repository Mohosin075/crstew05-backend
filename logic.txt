1. Product Vision
The Coach Profile (TCP) is intended to become the professional identity, recruiting marketplace, and career network for
football. The long-term product should connect coaches with collegiate programs, professional teams, recruiting agencies,
and football organizations while maintaining a trusted, verified professional graph of the football coaching ecosystem.
The initial product should focus on a strong core loop: Create Profile → Get Verified → Build Network → Discover Opportunities
→ Express Interest → Get Discovered → Communicate → Get Hired → Update Career → Invite Coaches.
2. Product Principles
• Fast time-to-value: a coach should be able to enter the product quickly without completing a long application.
• Professional identity: the Coach Profile should become a durable career record rather than a temporary job-search
profile.
• Two-sided marketplace: coaches are one side; programs, teams, agencies, and organizations are the other.
• Structured data first: critical career and recruiting information must be stored in structured fields for search, matching,
analytics, and future AI.
• Trust and verification: identity, experience, credentials, and organizations should have distinct verification states.
• Privacy by design: recruiting activity, internal notes, and sensitive information must respect permissions.
• Network effects: referrals, shareable profiles, coaching relationships, and program discovery should compound user
growth.
• Build for scale: architecture should support future enterprise recruiting tools without requiring a core rebuild.
3. Role Specifications
1. COACH
Purpose
The Coach is the primary supply-side user. The product should help coaches build a verified professional identity, network,
discover opportunities, express interest in programs, communicate with recruiters/programs, and advance their careers.
Core access
Free account with statewide networking and core profile functionality. Pro membership at $19.88/month unlocks nationwide
access, expanded visibility, advanced discovery/matching, and other premium career features.
Key features
• Account creation with email/password plus Google/Apple sign-in where technically feasible.
• Fast onboarding with the ability to Skip and Complete Later.
• Resume upload/import to pre-populate coaching history, education, credentials, positions, and experience.
• Professional profile with photo, headline, bio, coaching philosophy, experience, education, credentials, recruiting
footprint, expertise, career preferences, and career history.
• Verified identity/experience/credential badges.
• Shareable public profile URL with privacy controls.
• Coach search and statewide/nationwide networking.
• Opportunity discovery with filters and personalized match scores.
• Express Interest in a program or opportunity with selectable information to share and optional message.
• Messages with programs, teams, recruiters, and other coaches subject to permissions.
• Saved opportunities.
• Profile views and career analytics.
• Notifications for opportunity matches, messages, profile activity, verification, and recruiting activity.
• Referral/invite functionality.
• Career status: Actively Looking, Open to Opportunities, Currently Coaching, Not Looking, etc.
• Discreet recruiting/visibility controls so a coach can limit who sees recruiting activity.
• Career history that can be updated when a coach changes positions.
• Future-ready areas for coaching tree, recommendations, video introduction, AI career assistant, and paid coaching
services.
Coach flow
• Create account → choose Coach → verify email → basic information.
• Create a minimum viable profile and enter the app quickly.
• Progressively complete profile sections from Home/Profile.
• Verify identity/experience/credentials.
• Choose career status and preferences.
• Browse opportunities and coaches/programs.
• View opportunity/program → Express Interest or Apply/Contact.
• Program/recruiter receives the coach and can save/contact/manage the candidate.
• Coach receives notifications and communicates through Messages.
• Coach updates career status when hired and adds the new position to career history.
Coach permissions
• Edit own profile and career history.
• Control profile visibility and contact preferences.
• Choose what information is shared when expressing interest.
• Send/receive messages according to platform rules.
• Save/remove opportunities and profiles.
• Delete account and request data export where required.
• Cannot edit program/team/recruiter records or other coaches’ profiles.
2. COLLEGIATE ATHLETIC PROGRAM
Purpose
A verified college athletic program is the primary institutional recruiting user. It should be able to build an organization
presence, post opportunities, discover coaches, manage candidates, and communicate with coaches.
Core access
Program accounts should have an organization profile and role-based access. Pricing can be subscription-based, with a
future tiered structure such as Recruiting and Recruiting Pro. The exact commercial packaging can be finalized separately
from the core product architecture.
Key features
• Verified college/program profile with school, conference/level, location, sports/football program information, website,
logo, and authorized users.
• Post and manage coaching opportunities.
• Search and filter coaches by position, level, experience, geography, recruiting footprint, expertise, credentials, career
status, and other structured fields.
• View coach profiles subject to privacy settings.
• Receive and manage Express Interest submissions.
• Save/favorite coaches.
• Message coaches.
• Candidate pipeline: Discovered → Saved → Contacted → Interested → Interview → Offer → Hired.
• Internal notes and candidate management for authorized team members.
• Match scores and recommended coaches.
• Dashboard showing open roles, candidate activity, profile views, and recruiting activity.
• Multiple staff/user accounts with permissions.
• Program badge/verification indicator.
Program flow
• Create organization account → verify organization → create program profile → add authorized users.
• Post opportunity → configure requirements → publish.
• Search/discover coaches → review profiles → save/contact/express recruiting interest.
• Receive coach Express Interest → review profile → move candidate through pipeline.
• Message/interview/manage candidate → Offer → Hired.
• Update hired coach’s record where appropriate and maintain the program’s recruiting history.
Program permissions
• Organization Admin: full organization and user management.
• Recruiting/Staff User: search, save, message, manage candidates, and post/manage opportunities if permitted.
• Read-only user: view recruiting information without changing records.
• Program users cannot edit a coach’s profile, experience, credentials, or career history.
• Only authorized organization admins can add/remove program users.
3. PROFESSIONAL TEAM
Purpose
Professional teams should use the same core recruiting infrastructure as colleges but with professional-team-specific fields,
workflows, and privacy requirements.
Key features
• Verified professional team/organization profile.
• Post coaching and football staff opportunities.
• Search/filter coaches using structured professional criteria.
• Candidate pipeline and recruiting workspace.
• Save, compare, contact, and manage candidates.
• Internal notes and multi-user recruiting access.
• Candidate match scoring.
• Private recruiting activity where appropriate.
• Organization analytics and recruiting activity dashboard.
Professional team flow
• Create team account → organization verification → configure users/roles.
• Create/post opportunity → define requirements.
• Search/recommendations → review coaches → save/contact.
• Receive Express Interest → evaluate → interview → offer → hired.
• Maintain historical recruiting records and organization activity.
Permissions
• Team Admin: full organization/user/recruiting management.
• Authorized Recruiting/Football Operations users: candidate management and messaging.
• Read-only users: view permitted records.
• Sensitive recruiting notes should be visible only to authorized team members.
4. RECRUITING AGENCY / SEARCH FIRM
Purpose
Recruiting agencies/search firms should be able to manage multiple searches and source candidates across the verified
coach network.
Key features
• Verified recruiting agency profile.
• Multiple active searches/jobs.
• Search across coaches using advanced filters.
• Save candidates to specific searches.
• Candidate pipeline per search.
• Internal notes and collaboration.
• Message candidates.
• Track candidate status.
• Candidate recommendations/match scoring.
• Agency dashboard showing active searches and candidate activity.
• Ability to manage multiple client organizations where applicable.
Agency flow
• Create agency account → verification → add authorized recruiters.
• Create client/search/project → define position requirements.
• Search/discover coaches → save candidates to search → contact.
• Manage candidate through pipeline → interview → submit/present → hired/closed.
• Maintain separate client/search data and permissions.
Permissions
• Agency Admin: manage agency, recruiters, searches, and permissions.
• Recruiter: manage assigned searches and candidates.
• Recruiters should only see client/search information for which they are authorized.
• Client-sensitive notes and candidate information must not be exposed across unrelated searches.
5. ORGANIZATION
Purpose
Organizations are non-team entities such as coaching associations, conferences, camps, clinics, training organizations,
media organizations, nonprofits, or other football-related groups.
Key features
• Verified organization profile.
• Organization badge.
• Ability to post approved opportunities, events, camps, clinics, and relevant announcements.
• Ability to discover/search coaches where permitted.
• Ability to contact coaches subject to permissions.
• Community/content capabilities in future phases.
• Potential sponsorship/advertising placements.
• Member/referral programs.
• Organization analytics.
Organization flow
• Create organization account → select organization type → verification.
• Build organization profile → add authorized users.
• Publish opportunities/events/content.
• Discover coaches or invite coaches to relevant programs/events.
• Manage responses and communications.
Permissions
• Organization Admin: full profile/content/user management.
• Authorized Staff: create/manage permitted content and communications.
• Organizations do not receive unrestricted access to private coach information.
4. Cross-Platform Requirements
A. PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE
• The five roles must share a common account/identity system but have role-specific dashboards, permissions, and
workflows.
• Use structured data for Coach, Organization, Program/Team, Opportunity, Experience, Credential, Connection, Interest,
Message, Application/Candidate, Search, and Hire objects.
• Do not store critical recruiting information only as free-form text. Structured fields are required for search, filtering,
matching, analytics, and future AI.
• Build role-based access control from the beginning.
• Design the database so a coach can have multiple historical positions and relationships with multiple organizations.
• Separate public profile data, private coach data, recruiting activity, internal organization notes, and sensitive account
information.
• Build APIs/services so future web and mobile clients can use the same backend.
B. SEARCH & MATCHING
• Search must support structured filters rather than keyword-only search.
• Coach filters should include position, level, years of experience, geography, recruiting footprint, expertise, credentials,
career status, education, and other relevant structured attributes.
• Opportunity filters should include position, level, location, organization type, experience requirements, and career
preferences.
• Match scores should be explainable at a high level—for example: Position match, Experience match, Geography match,
Recruiting match, and Level match.
• Do not create a simplistic 'best coach' ranking. Use match/fit indicators instead.
C. VERIFICATION & TRUST
• Identity verification should be separate from experience verification.
• Credentials should support document upload and verification status.
• Verified organization/program accounts should be clearly distinguished from unverified accounts.
• Verification status should be stored as structured data with timestamps and, where applicable, verification source.
• Build for moderation/reporting of fraudulent profiles, impersonation, spam, and inappropriate content.
D. PRIVACY & RECRUITING DISCRETION
• Coaches must control profile visibility.
• Coaches should be able to limit recruiting visibility and contact.
• Current employer information and recruiting activity should not automatically be exposed to everyone.
• Organization internal notes must never be visible to coaches or unrelated organizations.
• Messages and recruiting records must respect role permissions.
• Include account deletion, privacy settings, and appropriate data export/deletion workflows.
E. NETWORK EFFECTS & GROWTH
• Referral/invite system should be part of the initial architecture.
• Coach profiles should have shareable URLs.
• Founding Coach/Founding Member badges should be supported.
• Track referral source and referral relationships.
• Allow coaches to invite members of their coaching network.
• Track acquisition source, activation, profile completion, connection creation, opportunity views, Express Interest events,
and conversion to Pro.
F. MONETIZATION
• Coach Free: statewide networking/core profile and limited discovery.
• Coach Pro: $19.88/month for nationwide access and premium career/networking functionality.
• Programs, Professional Teams, Recruiting Agencies, and Organizations should be architected for paid subscriptions
and/or premium features.
• Do not hard-code pricing into the backend in a way that prevents future plans, trials, promotional pricing, annual plans,
or enterprise contracts.
• Support feature entitlements by subscription plan rather than creating separate code paths for each price.
• Potential future revenue streams include enterprise recruiting software, premium search, sponsored opportunities,
advertising/sponsorships, paid coaching services, and transaction fees.
G. NOTIFICATION SYSTEM
• Notifications should be event-driven and support in-app notifications first, with email/push as appropriate.
• Events should include new message, opportunity match, profile view where permitted, Express Interest, verification,
candidate movement, saved opportunity changes, and relevant organization activity.
• Users should be able to control notification preferences.
H. ANALYTICS
• Track activation and conversion funnel: account created → profile started → profile completed → verified → first connection
→ first opportunity view → first Express Interest → first message → subscription.
• Track marketplace health: active coaches, active programs, active opportunities, coach-program connections,
applications/interests, interviews, hires, and retention.
• Do not rely solely on vanity metrics such as downloads.
5. DAILY ENGAGEMENT & COACHING NETWORK FEED
The platform must not function only as a periodic job-search tool. The long-term objective is to make The Coach Profile a
daily-use professional network for football coaches. The product should have a continuously updated Coaching Network
feed that gives coaches a reason to open the app every day, even when they are not actively looking for a job.
A. Daily Engagement Engine
• Create a dedicated Coaching Network / Feed experience separate from the career dashboard.
• The feed should continuously update with relevant coaching content, professional activity, opportunities, news, and
discussions.
• The feed must be personalized based on coaching position, level, geography, experience, career status, network,
followed users, interests, opportunities viewed, and engagement history.
• Support two feed modes: Following (content from people/accounts the coach follows) and For You (algorithmically
recommended content).
• The feed should create a daily habit without becoming a generic sports-fan news feed.
B. Content Types
• Coach posts: career updates, coaching philosophy, practice ideas, recruiting insights, accomplishments, questions,
photos, and videos.
• Coaching News: coaching hires, promotions, staff changes, openings, retirements, major coaching industry
developments, and relevant rules/industry updates.
• Coaching Carousel: a dedicated stream of coaching moves such as new hires, openings, promotions, departures, and
staff changes.
• Opportunity cards: relevant open coaching positions should appear naturally inside the feed with match scores and
direct access to the opportunity.
• Network activity: new positions, certifications, achievements, profile updates, and other professional milestones from
coaches in the user's network.
• Coaching discussions: professional questions and conversations focused on coaching, recruiting, player development,
schemes, staff management, and career development.
• Coaching IQ: short educational posts/videos such as scheme breakdowns, technique instruction, recruiting tips,
practice organization, and coaching lessons.
• Trending in Coaching: topics and discussions receiving significant engagement across the network.
• Coach Spotlight / Coach of the Day or Week: highlighted coaches to encourage participation and reward quality
profiles/content.
• Program and organization content: verified programs, teams, agencies, and organizations can publish relevant
opportunities, announcements, clinics, camps, events, and professional updates.
C. Follow vs. Connect
• Distinguish between Follow and Connect. Follow means a user wants to see another user's content. Connect means
users want a professional networking relationship.
• This distinction should support a much larger social graph without forcing every content relationship to become a formal
connection.
D. Personalized Feed
• For You recommendations should use structured profile information and behavior.
• Prioritize coaching opportunities, news, people, discussions, and educational content relevant to the user's role and
career goals.
• Personalization inputs should include position, coaching level, geography, recruiting footprint, expertise, career status,
desired positions, followed accounts, connections, opportunities viewed/saved, searches, and content engagement.
• The architecture should support a future AI-powered recommendation engine without requiring a redesign of the feed.
E. Content → Network → Opportunity Flywheel
• The feed must connect content to TCP's career marketplace: Feed → Discover Coach/Program → Follow/Connect →
Discover Opportunity → Express Interest → Message → Interview/Hire → Career Update → New Content → More Network
Activity.
F. Coach Posting & Growth Incentives
• Allow coaches to publish posts and professional updates.
• Support achievements such as championships, promotions, certifications, new positions, clinics, speaking
engagements, and coaching milestones.
• Provide visibility incentives such as Coach Spotlight, profile boosts, or featured content where appropriate.
• Allow coaches to share their TCP profile and selected posts externally.
• Track referrals and attribute new users to coach invitations where applicable.
G. Communities / Groups — P1
• Support future communities such as Defensive Coordinators, Offensive Coordinators, DB Coaches, High School
Coaches, College Coaches, SEC Coaches, Texas Coaches, Recruiting Coordinators, and Strength & Conditioning.
• Communities should support posts, discussions, moderation, and discovery.
H. Short-Form Video — P1
• Plan for short-form coaching video content such as 60-second coaching tips, scheme breakdowns, practice clips,
recruiting advice, and coaching philosophy.
• Video should be architected as a future content type rather than required for initial launch.
I. Daily Home Experience
• Home should function as a personalized career dashboard with profile completion, profile views, recommended
opportunities, career activity, and alerts.
• Feed should function as the continuously changing social/professional engagement surface.
J. Feed Safety, Quality & Moderation
• Provide reporting and blocking tools.
• Support moderation of spam, impersonation, harassment, inappropriate content, and fraudulent recruiting activity.
• Verified organizations and coaches should be clearly identified.
• Do not allow the feed to become a generic sports-fan discussion board; content should remain centered on professional
football/coaching.
• Build content ranking and moderation controls so low-quality or abusive content does not dominate the network.
6. FOOTBALL INTELLIGENCE: SCORES, SCHEDULES & FOOTBALL UPDATES
To create a true daily-use habit, TCP should eventually include football information that coaches care about every day. This
should be positioned as Football Intelligence for Coaches—not as a generic sports-fan product. The experience should
connect scores, schedules, news, coaching changes, network activity, and opportunities back to the professional coaching
ecosystem.
A. Coverage
• NCAA Division I: FBS and FCS scores, schedules, rankings, conference information, and relevant news.
• NCAA Division II: scores, schedules, rankings, conference information, and relevant news.
• NCAA Division III: scores, schedules, rankings, conference information, and relevant news.
• Professional football: NFL initially, with the architecture flexible enough to support additional professional leagues in the
future.
• Coverage should prioritize football levels that are relevant to TCP's coaching users rather than attempting to cover every
sport.
B. Football Section
• Create a dedicated Football area that can show LIVE, Scores, Schedule, Rankings/Standings, News, and Coaching
Moves.
• During game windows, surface live and final scores prominently.
• Allow users to follow teams, conferences, programs, or levels so relevant games are prioritized.
• Allow users to receive notifications for selected games, teams, programs, or major coaching events.
• Keep the Football area distinct enough to be useful while also surfacing relevant football cards inside the personalized
Coaching Network feed.
C. Personalized Football Feed
• Prioritize games and football updates based on the coach's profile, location, level, position, followed programs,
connections, and interests.
• Examples: a Texas high school coach may see Texas programs first; a college defensive coordinator may see college
defensive coaching news and relevant games; a professional coach may see NFL/professional updates first.
• Do not show an undifferentiated wall of scores. Personalization is essential to keeping the feed useful.
D. Connect Football Data to the Professional Network
• Football scores and events should link back to TCP programs, teams, coaches, and opportunities whenever relevant.
• When a program plays a game, users should be able to navigate from the game/program card to the program's TCP
profile.
• When appropriate, surface relevant TCP coaches associated with a program or coaching staff.
• Coaching changes should connect directly to affected programs and, where appropriate, to qualified coaches in the TCP
network.
• Example workflow: Coaching News → Program → Open Position → Matched Coaches → Express Interest → Recruiting
Pipeline.
E. Daily / Weekly Programming
• Weekday: coaching news, coaching carousel, opportunities, network activity, discussions, and educational content.
• Game days: LIVE football, followed teams, relevant scores, coaching updates, and opportunities.
• Post-game: results, relevant coaching takeaways, network discussion, and coaching news.
• Sunday/Monday professional cycle: NFL/professional updates, coaching movement, opportunity changes, and weekly
recap content.
F. Data Licensing / Technical Requirement
• Do not scrape or republish third-party sports sites without permission.
• Use a legitimate sports-data provider/API with commercial rights appropriate for the intended TCP product.
• Before implementation, evaluate providers for D1/FBS, FCS, D2, D3, NFL/pro coverage, live score latency, schedules,
standings, logos, statistics, licensing terms, API limits, and cost.
• The sports-data layer should be modular so TCP can change providers without rebuilding the product.
• Store normalized sports entities separately from TCP's internal Coach/Program/Organization entities, with stable
mapping between them.
G. Product Positioning
The objective is not to compete directly with ESPN or NFL.com as a general sports destination. TCP should provide Football
Intelligence for Coaches: football information that is personalized and directly connected to coaching careers, professional
networking, recruiting, and opportunities.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT DIRECTION — DAILY HABIT
The goal is for a coach to use TCP every day, not only when looking for a new job. The Coaching Network feed is therefore a
strategic product component, not an optional social feature. It should create a professional daily habit while continuously
feeding users back into networking, opportunities, recruiting, and career advancement. The feed should feel closer to a
professional coaching network than a generic sports social network.
IMPORTANT PRODUCT DIRECTION — FOOTBALL INTELLIGENCE
Football content should be treated as a daily-engagement engine that feeds the professional network, not as a separate
generic sports app. The desired loop is: Football Event → Content/Score → Coach Engagement → Discussion/Network →
Program Discovery → Opportunity → Recruiting → Hire → Career Update.

THE DAILY HABIT LOOP
Football/Coaching Update → Personalized Feed → Coach Engagement → Follow/Connect → Discussion/Content → Opportunity
Discovery → Express Interest → Recruiting Conversation → Hire/Career Update → New Network Activity → Repeat.
WHAT A COACH SHOULD SEE WHEN OPENING TCP
• Personalized Coaching Network Feed with continuously changing content.
• LIVE football scores and game status when games are being played.
• Upcoming schedules and results for followed/relevant teams and programs.
• D1/FBS, FCS, D2, D3, and professional football updates.
• Coaching News and Coaching Carousel: hires, promotions, openings, departures, retirements, and major staff changes.
• New coaching opportunities matched to the coach's profile.
• Updates from coaches, programs, teams, recruiting agencies, and organizations in the user's network.
• Trending coaching discussions and professional questions.
• Coaching IQ: short educational coaching content, scheme breakdowns, technique tips, recruiting advice, and practice
ideas.
• Who viewed your profile, who followed/connected with you, and relevant network activity.
• Personalized recommendations for coaches, programs, opportunities, and content.
• Notifications for important games, teams/programs followed, messages, opportunities, recruiting activity, and network
events.
CORE FEED STRUCTURE
The Feed should have two primary modes: Following and For You. Following shows content from coaches, programs,
organizations, teams, and other accounts the user follows. For You uses structured profile information and behavior to
personalize content, football updates, opportunities, discussions, and recommendations.
CONTENT TYPES REQUIRED IN THE FEED
• Coach posts and professional updates.
• Program/team/organization posts.
• Coaching News.
• Coaching Carousel.
• LIVE football scores and game cards.
• Final scores and game recaps.
• Upcoming schedules.
• Rankings/standings where licensed data permits.
• Relevant football news.
• Open coaching opportunities.
• Opportunity match cards with a Match Score.
• Network activity and career milestones.
• Professional discussions.
• Trending in Coaching.
• Coaching IQ educational content.
• Coach Spotlight / Coach of the Day or Week.
• Future short-form coaching video.
• Future communities/groups.
IMPORTANT: HOME VS. FEED
Home and Feed should have different purposes. Home is the coach's career command center: profile completion, profile
views, career analytics, recommended opportunities, recruiting activity, and alerts. Feed is the daily habit engine: football,
coaching news, people, discussions, educational content, opportunities, and network activity.
FOOTBALL INTELLIGENCE IS NOT A GENERIC SPORTS FEED
Do not build TCP as a general ESPN-style sports destination. Football content must be relevant to people who work in football
or want to work in football. The product should connect Football Event → Program → Coaches → Network → Opportunity →
Recruiting.
EXAMPLE DAILY EXPERIENCE
• Morning: coaching news, overnight staff changes, new opportunities, network activity, and Coaching IQ.
• During games: LIVE scores, followed teams, relevant programs, coaching updates, and discussions.
• After games: final scores, recaps, coaching takeaways, network discussions, and related opportunities.
• Sunday/Monday: professional football updates, coaching carousel activity, new openings, and weekly recaps.
• Every day: personalized recommendations, profile views, new connections, messages, and career activity.
DAILY ENGAGEMENT PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS
• Track feed impressions, content views, engagement, follows, connections, opportunity clicks, Express Interest events,
and return frequency.
• Build notifications and ranking infrastructure so the feed can continuously surface fresh, relevant content.
• Allow users to follow coaches, programs, teams, organizations, topics, and potentially conferences.
• Separate Follow from Connect.
• Provide reporting/blocking/moderation tools.
• Design the content system to support text, images, links, structured football cards, opportunity cards, and future video.
• Design the recommendation architecture so an AI-powered feed can be introduced later without rebuilding the content
system.
